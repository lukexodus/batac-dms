City Government of Batac · Ilocos Norte Current State
Section 2.1 — Current State
How Documents Move Today
City Hall's current document process is predominantly manual and paper-based.
🚶 Routing by Physical Presence

Documents are manually handed off between offices. Delays occur whenever a responsible person is absent or unavailable.

🔍 No Real-Time Visibility

No centralized view of where a document is in its lifecycle. Status requires manual inquiry — a phone call or a visit.

⏱ Slow Retrieval

Locating a specific document for audit or reference requires searching physical storage — unreliable and slow.

📋 ARTA Compliance Gap

RA 11032 processing time limits cannot be monitored or enforced without automated tracking systems.

📂 Version Confusion

Earlier document versions are sometimes confused with or replace final copies, creating compliance and accuracy risks.

⚠️ Records at Risk

Inconsistent archiving creates risk of document loss and non-compliance with COA retention requirements.

City Government of Batac · Ilocos Norte Mandate & Justification
Section 2.2 — Why This Is Necessary
Legal Obligation, Not Just Efficiency
RA 11032 — Ease of Doing Business Act

Legally enforceable maximum processing times: 3 days simple, 7 days complex, 20 days highly technical. Non-compliance carries penalties.

Public Accountability & Transparency

Citizens have a right to access public government records and know the status of their submissions. Digital infrastructure makes this feasible at scale.

Operational Scale

SP legislative output, executive issuances, departmental operations, 43 barangay transmittals, and citizen services have reached a volume where manual management is an operational liability.

COA Retention Compliance

Commission on Audit mandates specific retention schedules per document type. Without systematic archiving, the city is exposed to audit findings.

City Government of Batac · Ilocos Norte Legal Framework
Section 2.3 — Dual Source of Truth
Physical & Digital Records Coexist
📄 Physical Records
Legal Source of Truth

Official resolutions, ordinances, contracts, and signed orders remain legally authoritative in their physical, wet-ink-signed form until COA formally confirms the legal equivalence of digital records for each document category.

💻 This Platform
Operational Source of Truth

The system provides the digital record of what was processed, by whom, when, and in what sequence. It manages workflows, tracking, and visibility — not replacement of legal originals.

This is not a paperless initiative

LGU Batac will retain all physical originals post-digitization until formal regulatory confirmation is received from COA for each document category. Physical and digital records coexist throughout system operation.

Drafting, Routing & Approval
UC1 — Draft and Submit a Document

An encoder or staff creates a new document, attaches files, fills metadata, and formally submits to begin the workflow.

  • System assigns a tracking number
  • Files stored with full version history
  • Document handed to first workflow step
  • No series number at draft — assigned only on approval
No version is ever overwritten

Every upload creates a new version. All previous versions are retained and accessible to authorized users.

UC2 — Document Routing & Approval

An approver receives an assigned document in their queue, reviews it, and takes a formal action.

1
System assigns document to current step's designated approver
2
Approver receives in-app notification; item appears in queue
3
Action: Approve, Return for revision, or Reject (with written reason)
4
All actions permanently recorded in audit trail with timestamp

Citizens & Barangay Officials
UC5 — Citizen Submits & Tracks a Request

A Batac City resident submits a service request through the public portal and monitors progress — without a single visit to City Hall.

  • Register with name, phone, email + OTP verification
  • No account needed for status lookup — just the tracking number
  • View approved SP resolutions and ordinances publicly
  • Submit complaints; track their own submissions
Classification guardrails

Internal documents are never visible to the public. Document classification is enforced — not just displayed.

UC6 — Barangay Submits to City Hall

A Barangay Secretary digitally transmits a barangay resolution or endorsement to City Hall from their phone — no physical delivery to initiate.

  • Mobile-first design — all 43 barangay functions work via mobile browser
  • Offline queuing for intermittent connections
  • Access scoped to own barangay only — no cross-barangay visibility
  • City Hall logs, assigns tracking number, routes automatically
Phase 1 note

Barangay officials have no system login in Phase 1. Secretariat logs physically submitted documents on their behalf. Mobile access planned for Phase 1B.

UC9 — Search & Retrieve

Any authorized user can find a document in seconds — by type, office, date, status, or keyword.

  • Filter by type, office, date, status, classification
  • Results scoped to user's role and office
  • All versions accessible; current copy clearly identified
Access enforced, not just displayed

Confidential docs excluded from results unless user is on the explicit allowlist.

UC10 — Audit Log Review

Complete, unalterable action history of any document — every action, every actor, every timestamp.

  • Auth events, state changes, approvals, exports
  • Actor identity, IP, timestamp, session ID
  • Hash-chained: modification breaks chain
INSERT-only DB permissions

No UPDATE or DELETE possible, even for IT Administrators. Auditor role is read-only.

City Government of Batac · Ilocos Norte Provincial Review
Section 4.3 — Sangguniang Panlalawigan Review
Provincial Review — Automated Tracking

Outcome Types

OutcomeMeaning
VALIDApproved by Panlalawigan
VALID-IN-PARTPartially approved; some provisions invalid
RETURNEDReturned with objections
Referred to Committee30-day clock running
Operative-in-entiretyUsed for Appropriation Ordinances
Blank / 30 days elapsedDeemed approved per RA 7160 §56(d)

System Behavior

30-Day Auto-Timer

System automatically tracks SLA from transmission date. At day 30 with no response → status transitions to "Deemed Approved per RA 7160 §56(d)" and notifies SP Secretary for confirmation. No manual date tracking.

VALID-IN-PART Handling

Not auto-routed. SP Secretary chooses: (1) Resolve as-is with comment, (2) Route to Legal Office, or (3) Route back to committee for re-draft.

RETURNED Handling

High-priority alert. Step placed in manual review — immediate City Legal Office and Mayor coordination required.

City Government of Batac · Ilocos Norte Platform Administration
Use Case 8 — Platform Administration
Configurable Without Developer Involvement

What Admins Can Configure

  • Workflow steps, transitions, and branching conditions
  • Assignee rules per workflow step
  • SLA thresholds and escalation targets
  • Notification templates
  • Document classification and retention schedules
  • User accounts, roles, and office assignments
  • Designation delegation records (time-bound, authority-scoped)
Versioned — safe to change

Changes create a new workflow version. Documents already in progress continue under their original version. No in-flight document is disrupted.

Designation / Authority Transfer

Time-bound delegation records

When a Designation is logged (e.g., Vice Mayor designated Acting Mayor), the system creates a delegation_grant record. All workflow steps are automatically reassigned to the designated person for the duration.

Manual confirmation required

Platform Administrator manually confirms delegation scope to prevent erroneous authority transfers. Delegations expire automatically at end date. High-frequency operation: 10+ VM-as-Acting-Mayor designations per year.

Legally mandated steps are locked

The system validates that SP Ordinance and Resolution workflows include all legally required steps. Attempting to publish a workflow definition without these steps is blocked.
