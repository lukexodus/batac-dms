### Referral-Review-Submission-Readings-Timeline Workflow

It is the committee that schedules a session, not the SP secretary.

They add it to their committee report if they need a hearing or not. That is not their final report. After the meeting, they will have a new report and then pass it to the session. It is the committee that creates the final report.

The secretariat just logs. They receive the notices. The Secretariat logs the hearing schedules.

The committee schedules the second reading.

Secretariat schedules the first reading.

In one week, the session is Tuesday. Cutoff is Thursday. When committees are referred to Tuesday. They should finish their committee reports before Thursday finishes.

Even if committees have not submitted their report, these are marked red in the Order of Business document.

The Secretariat receives drafts. If they receive before the cutoff, they include it in the Order of Business document. Then it is scheduled for the session next week.

1st reading. VM refers to Committee/s. Title is passed. 

Tuesday is the Session. 

1st reading and 2nd reading can still happen in the same session. (`Certified Urgent`. This happens frequently)

It is the Mayor that determines if a hearing is urgent or not. If it is, it goes next directly to 2nd reading.
